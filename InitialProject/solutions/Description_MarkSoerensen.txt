   Loss function and value on validation set:
      K-Means: Inertia (sum of squared distances)
      Feature Selection AE: MSE (Mean Squared Error) for reconstruction (trained on training split of candidate features).
      Anomaly Detection AE: MSE (Mean Squared Error) for reconstruction (trained on training split of selected features).
